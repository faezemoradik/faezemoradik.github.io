I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Toronto, working with [Prof. Scott Sanner](https://d3m.mie.utoronto.ca/members/ssanner/) on LLM post-training (reinforcement learning, test-time learning, reasoning) and memory-based agentic AI.

I completed my Ph.D. in the ECE Department at the University of Toronto in 2025, advised by [Prof. Ben Liang](https://www.comm.utoronto.ca/~liang/index.html) and [Prof. Min Dong](https://sites.google.com/ontariotechu.net/dong), on differentially private machine learning systems and optimization algorithms for distributed learning systems. Before that, I received my M.Sc. (2021) and B.Sc. (2018) in Electrical Engineering from the University of Tehran.


Research interests
======
* Large Language Models (LLMs)
* LLM post-training
* Agentic AI
* Distributed learning
* Differential privacy
